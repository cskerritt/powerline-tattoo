The Studio

A Private Studio Built Around You

A tattoo is one of the most powerful forms of personal expression you'll ever commit to — it stays with you for life. At Powerline Tattoo, our mission is simple: deliver the best possible tattoo alongside the best possible experience for every single client.

As a private studio, we offer a level of one-on-one attention, comfort, and exclusivity that a typical walk-in shop simply cannot match. We don't keep public hours or accept walk-ins — instead, we've built a streamlined process that makes it easy to reach out, talk through your ideas, and ultimately get the tattoo you've been envisioning.

Your journey begins with the tattoo request form on our contact page. That gives us the basics and lets us pair you with the artist best suited to your project. From there, we'll either schedule an in-person consultation or continue refining the details over email. Once the concept is locked in and both artist and client are comfortable, you leave a deposit and book your session.

Our staff is a small, hand-picked group of professionals chosen for the excellence they bring to their craft. Powerline artists have earned countless awards and been featured in international publications.

You won't find pre-drawn flash hanging on our walls waiting to be picked and stuck. We collaborate with you on the design from the ground up. Bring reference material, bring rough ideas, or come with nothing more than a feeling — we'll work with you no matter how developed your concept is.
