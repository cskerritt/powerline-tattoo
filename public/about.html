The Studio

Built on Passion, Driven by Craft

Powerline Tattoo was founded with a single mission: to deliver exceptional custom tattoos in a welcoming, professional environment. From day one, we've built our reputation on honest consultations, clean work, and art that lasts a lifetime.

Every piece we create is a one-of-a-kind collaboration between artist and client. We take the time to understand your vision, refine your concept, and translate it into something you'll be proud to carry on your skin forever. No flash templates — only original, intentional work.

We hold ourselves to the highest sterilization and safety standards in the industry. Our studio is fully autoclave-equipped, and every station is set up fresh for each client. Your health and comfort are never an afterthought.
