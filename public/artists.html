Meet the Artists

Talented, dedicated artists — each with a distinct voice and a commitment to exceptional work.

Evan Olin

Realism & Color

Owner • 15+ years experience

View Profile ↓

Owner and lead artist at Powerline Tattoo. Evan holds a Bachelor's Degree in Illustration from the Rhode Island School of Design, and brings a fine-art sensibility to every piece. His award-winning photorealistic color work has earned him recognition at conventions up and down the East Coast. Favorite subjects include anything dark or horror-related, wildlife and animals, portraits, and anything he can render in his realistic style.

Book Evan @evanolintattoo

Mike Ledoux

Illustrative Realism

Resident Artist • 20+ years experience

View Profile ↓

Tattooing in Rhode Island studios since 2003, Mike holds a Master's Degree in Art Education from Rhode Island College. While experienced in a wide variety of techniques, his strength lies in his illustrative style — fusing realism with inventive physics and creative secondary lighting. Think Dr. Seuss meets John Audubon, or Salvador Dali sitting on Norman Rockwell's lap.

Book Mike @mledouxtattoo

Mikey Romasco

Black & Grey Realism & Portraiture

Resident Artist • 15+ years experience

View Profile ↓

A lifelong Massachusetts resident, Mikey developed a passion for art at a young age — starting with graphite drawings and portraiture. Tattooing since 2009, his main strength is black and grey realism and portraiture, though he also works in an illustrative neo-traditional style. Whatever the piece, his goal is to create something unique that flows with the body.

Book Mikey @mikeyffresh

Andy Large

Dynamic Realism & Surrealism

Resident Artist

View Profile ↓

Born and raised in Rhode Island, Andy focuses on making dynamic realism tattoos with surreal elements. Favorite subjects include nature, animals, flowers, human portraiture, and space. He mostly takes on larger projects, enjoying the challenge of creating big, interesting compositions with complex detail. With a background in illustration and painting, he brings those influences into every piece.

Book Andy @andylargetattoo
