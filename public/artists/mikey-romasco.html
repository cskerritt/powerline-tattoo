Mikey Romasco

Black & Grey Realism & Portraiture — Resident Artist, 15+ Years

My name is Mike Romasco and I am a lifelong resident of Massachusetts. Since a very young age I developed a great interest in art and could usually be found with a pencil in hand. I mainly focused on graphite drawings. As time went on, my attention began to lean toward portraiture. I have been tattooing since 2009. My main goal is to create something unique that flows with the body.
