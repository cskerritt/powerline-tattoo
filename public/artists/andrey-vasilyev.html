Andrey Vasilyev

Color Neo-Traditional — Resident Artist

My name is Andrey Vasilyev! My preferred tattoo style is color neo-traditional, often with bold lines and vibrant colors. I focus on illustrating beautiful, unique designs that stand out. I enjoy doing illustrative portraits, flowers, daggers, and skulls. I use my drawing and illustration skills to create custom work as well as pre-drawn designs, which you can find on my Instagram @andreytattooing!
