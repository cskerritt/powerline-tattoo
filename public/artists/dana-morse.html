Dana Morse

Versatile — Traditional to Realism — Resident Artist, 18+ Years

I'm Dana Morse, a tattoo artist with over 18 years of experience and a passion for creating custom, one-of-a-kind artwork. My journey began in Saint Johnsbury, Vermont, where I started tattooing the day after graduating high school at 18. Since then, my career has taken me across the country and even to the other side of the world, tattooing and connecting with amazing clients and artists along the way. I love the versatility of tattooing and enjoy working in multiple styles, from bold traditional to intricate realism and everything in between. One of my favorite challenges is blending styles to craft unique designs that truly represent my clients' stories and visions. No idea is too big, too small, or too unconventional — I'm here to make your dream tattoo a reality.
