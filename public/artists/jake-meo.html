Jake Meo

American Traditional — Resident Artist

Jake Meo brings bold, bright American traditional straight out of Providence. Tattooing since 2019, he's earned his name with large-scale classics, heavy saturation, and designs that respect the roots without copying the past. While American traditional is his foundation, Jake is happy to tattoo all styles — bringing the same focus, care, and attention to every piece, no matter the approach.
