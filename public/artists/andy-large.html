Andy Large

Dynamic Realism & Surrealism — Resident Artist

Hey, I'm Andy Large, born and raised in Rhode Island. I focus on making really dynamic realism tattoos with some surreal elements. Some subjects I enjoy tattooing include nature, animals, flowers, human portraiture, and space. I mostly take on larger projects — I enjoy the challenge of creating big, interesting compositions that look well thought out, with a lot of complex detail. Having a background in illustration and painting, I try to bring those influences into my work. My interests are pretty eclectic, from movies, anime, and comics to tabletop gaming. It's a dream come true incorporating the stuff I love into my work.
