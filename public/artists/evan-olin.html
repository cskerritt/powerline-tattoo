Evan Olin

Realism & Color — Owner & Lead Artist, 15+ Years

My name is Evan Olin. I enjoy taking my classical art background and applying it to my tattoos. I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Illustration from the Rhode Island School of Design. My tattoos often take on a painterly, realistic style. Some of my favorite subjects include anything dark or horror-related, wildlife and animals, portraits, and anything else I can render in my realistic style. My goal is to deliver one-of-a-kind tattoos that my customers can be proud to wear and show off for the rest of their lives.
