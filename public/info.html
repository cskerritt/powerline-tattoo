Info

Everything you need to know — before, during, and after your tattoo.

Do you accept walk-ins? +
No — we are an appointment-only studio. This allows us to give every client our full attention and ensure the best possible result. Please use the Book Now page to schedule a consultation or appointment.
How much does a tattoo cost? +
Pricing depends on size, placement, detail, and the time required. We provide custom quotes during your consultation — there's no one-size-fits-all rate. Reach out via the contact page or book a free consultation to discuss your idea and get an accurate estimate.
Do I need to leave a deposit? +
Yes. A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your appointment. It will be applied toward the total cost of your tattoo. If you need to reschedule, please give us at least 48 hours' notice — deposits may be forfeited for late cancellations or no-shows.
How should I prepare for my appointment? +
Get a good night's sleep, eat a full meal, and stay well-hydrated before your session. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed. Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours beforehand — it thins the blood and can affect healing.
Can I bring a friend? +
One guest is welcome to accompany you. Out of respect for other clients and our artists' focus, additional guests are asked to wait outside. Please do not bring children to the studio.
What forms of payment do you accept? +
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, and payments via Square. Tips are always appreciated and can be given in any of these forms.

Keep It Clean

Gently wash with unscented antibacterial soap 2–3 times daily. Pat dry — never rub.

✪

Moisturize

Apply a thin layer of unscented lotion or tattoo aftercare balm once the skin is dry. Less is more.

☀

Avoid Sun

Keep your new tattoo out of direct sunlight for at least 3 weeks. Once healed, use SPF 50+ to preserve vibrancy.

♨

No Soaking

Avoid baths, pools, hot tubs, and open water for 2–3 weeks. Showers are fine — just keep it brief and gentle.

⚠

Don't Pick or Scratch

Peeling and itching are normal. Resist the urge to pick — it can pull out ink and cause scarring.

☎

Contact Us

Any signs of unusual redness, swelling, or discharge? Reach out immediately. We're here to help.
