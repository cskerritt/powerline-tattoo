Info

Everything you need to know — before, during, and after your tattoo.

Do you accept walk-ins? +
As a private studio we do not keep walk-in hours. All consultations and tattoos are done by appointment only.
How old do I need to be to get tattooed? +
At least 18 years of age, with a valid photo ID (license, state ID, passport). A photocopy is required for health department records.
How do I go about making a tattoo appointment? +
Fill out the tattoo request form on the contact page. An artist suited to your request will be assigned. You may consult in-person or via email. A deposit is required when scheduling.
How does pricing work? +
For the most part, our artists charge by the hour, however some tattoos are priced by the piece. The artist provides an estimate before booking.
How should I prepare for my appointment? +
Get a good night's sleep, eat a good meal beforehand, wear appropriate clothing, keep good personal hygiene, and relax.
Can I bring someone with me? +
No, unfortunately we are not allowing guests to attend your appointment with you, unless they are also getting tattooed.
What forms of payment do you accept? +
We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, and Discover) and cash. There is a 4% charge added to any purchase made with a credit card. Checks are not accepted.
Should I tip my tattoo artist? +
Tipping is a common practice if you are happy with your tattoo. A suggested range is 15-20%, though any amount is appreciated and not mandatory.
☍

Bandaging

Leave the bandage on for approximately 2 hours, but no longer than 5 hours.

✪

Cleaning

Use mild antibacterial liquid hand soap (fragrance-free). Gently wash with your hands only — no loofahs or washcloths. Pat dry with clean paper towels.

♨

Moisturizing

Apply Hustle Butter or Aquaphor healing ointment thinly, then blot excess. The product should not be visible on the surface. Apply twice daily throughout healing.

☀

Sun Protection

Do not tan or expose your new tattoo to direct sunlight while healing. Use high SPF sunblock after it's fully healed.

⚠

Don't Pick

Let any flakes or scabs come off on their own. Picking prematurely will pull color out.

☎

Medical Alert

If you experience excessive redness, pain, rash, puss, or oozing, contact your artist immediately.
